Account Deletion Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

VegOps: Angithi Operation App allows authorized users to request permanent deletion of their account.

How to Request Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion through:

  • In-app account deletion feature (when available).
  • Request submitted to company administration.
  • Email request sent to admin@punjabiangihti.com.

What Happens When an Account Is Deleted

Upon approval and processing of an account deletion request:

  • User authentication credentials may be removed from Firebase Authentication.
  • Personal account information may be permanently deleted.
  • Access to VegOps services will be revoked.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain operational records may be retained for legitimate business, compliance, auditing, security, and reporting purposes, including:

  • Historical inspection submissions.
  • Operational evidence records.
  • Task completion history.
  • Compliance and audit records.

Retained records are preserved only where necessary for operational or legal requirements.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are generally processed within 30 days of verification.
