Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

VegOps: Angithi Operation App ("VegOps") is an internal operational application developed for Vegorama Punjabi Angithi to manage outlet operations, task assignments, inspections, submissions, evidence collection, and operational workflows.

Information We Collect

  • Phone number used for authentication.
  • User profile information including name, role, department, and assigned outlet.
  • Operational task submissions and inspection records.
  • Images and evidence uploaded as part of operational activities.
  • Application usage information required for security, troubleshooting, and service improvement.

Camera Access

VegOps may request camera access to capture operational evidence, inspection photographs, and documentation required for company processes.

Notifications

VegOps may send reminders and operational notifications related to assigned tasks, inspections, and workflow activities.

How Information Is Used

Collected information is used solely for:

  • Authentication and account management.
  • Operational task assignment and tracking.
  • Inspection and compliance monitoring.
  • Evidence collection and record keeping.
  • Internal reporting and operational analytics.
  • Security and system administration.

Data Storage

Information may be securely stored using Firebase services including Authentication, Firestore Database, Cloud Storage, and related Google Cloud infrastructure.

Analytics

The application may utilize Firebase and associated platform services for operational monitoring, diagnostics, reliability improvements, and application maintenance.

Data Sharing

Information collected through VegOps is used exclusively for internal business operations. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are implemented to protect operational and user information from unauthorized access.

Your Rights

Authorized users may request access, correction, or deletion of their account information subject to company operational requirements and applicable laws.
